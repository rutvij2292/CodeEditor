PHP built-in stubs for Kode's native PHP engine.

Modifications made by scripts/make-php-stubs.py (changed files are therefore NOT the upstream files):
  * only a subset of extensions is kept; every class / interface / trait / enum is split into its own file,
    functions and constants are grouped per upstream file;
  * JetBrains attributes are resolved for PHP 8.3 (elements not available there are removed, LanguageLevelTypeAware
    becomes a plain type) and removed; function bodies are emptied; PHPDoc is shortened and stripped of HTML and links;
  * index.txt is generated.
